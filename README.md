Girls Garden Field Manual

An English strategy reference for マブラヴ ガールズガーデン / Muv-Luv Girls Garden (マブガル).

One self-contained HTML file. Download it and open it in any browser — no server, no build step, no network needed. Everything (art, icons, fonts fallbacks, data) is embedded.

What's in it

Fourteen sections, navigated as a register — one at a time, with an All tab that reveals everything for Ctrl+F and printing.

Read first — the six things that decide everything, including the limit-break trap that costs new players the most.
Combat, attributes, status effects — the attribute cycle, the type triangle, and all seven status ailments with who inflicts them.
Growth, affinity — the 14-step rarity ladder, Level Link, verified affinity gains.
Maze — route unlocks, the five-step relic ladder, the full relic unlock tables by commander rank / depth / run condition, all 24 artifacts, the 12 type-equipment parts, and the eight Unexplored-Maze threat modifiers.
Modes, daily, gacha, walls — the daily loop, rates, and the three walls that stop everyone.
Tier board — 94 cards with their own art and their real rarity, attribute, type and role. Filter by list, by base/seasonal, by status effect, or search. Click any card for a detail panel.
Choices — a searchable lookup for every date answer.
Sources — every claim's origin, the term audit, and the revision history.
Where the data comes from

Tier placements are gamerch's, compiled 1 September 2026, alongside other Japanese community wikis — all listed in the Sources section.

Everything else — rarity, attribute, type, role, relics, artifacts, equipment, unlock tables, and all artwork — is read out of the game client's own MasterData and asset bundles, not copied from a wiki. Where a fan wiki and the game disagree, the game wins, and the disagreement is documented.

English translations of card titles are the author's own; the game does not provide them. Anything unverified is flagged in place rather than smoothed over.
